# Code of Conduct

BhoomiAI contributors are expected to maintain a professional, respectful, and collaborative environment.

## Expected Behaviour

- communicate respectfully and constructively
- focus criticism on technical work rather than individuals
- respect farmer privacy, institutional confidentiality, licensing, and data provenance
- avoid publishing unsafe agricultural instructions, credentials, or sensitive operational information
- document uncertainty and limitations in agricultural guidance

## Unacceptable Behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate publication of sensitive data, unsafe manipulation of project controls, or knowingly misleading claims about agricultural, security, or benchmark results are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may moderate or remove contributions that violate these expectations.
